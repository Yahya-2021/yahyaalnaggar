01 / ABOUT

A research career
rooted in curiosity.

My research is dedicated to understanding the biological and environmental factors that shape insect health, with a particular focus on honey bees and other pollinators.

At the Department of Zoology, Faculty of Science, Tanta University, I combine experimental entomology, molecular approaches, and collaborative research to investigate how chemical and biological stressors affect insect physiology, immunity, and survival.

My academic path has included research and scientific collaborations in Egypt, Canada, the United States, Germany, and other international research settings. I value interdisciplinary work that connects fundamental biology with practical solutions for pollinator health and sustainable agriculture.
